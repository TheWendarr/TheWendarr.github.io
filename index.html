About Me

I am an active-duty U.S. Army Geospatial Engineer Sergeant (12Y) with hands-on experience in terrain analysis, geospatial intelligence production, and mission planning. My military career has sharpened my ability to deliver precise, actionable geospatial products under real-world constraints, and I am now channeling that expertise into a civilian career at the intersection of GIS and software engineering.

I am completing a B.A.S. in Geospatial Intelligence & Analysis at Delta State University. Outside of work and school, I build geospatial tools in Python and Rust, run a self-hosted homelab, and am always looking for ways to automate and visualize.

Relevant courses: Web GIS • Remote Sensing • Data Science & Visualization • Cartography.
Interests: Geospatial Software Engineering, Cartography, Privacy-Focused and Open Source Infrastructure
